---
description: Book an interpreter: the client, the language, onsite, video or phone, the setting (medical, legal, court, community, business), the time and how long.
---

1. Get from the operator: who, language, mode, setting, date and time, how long, the address and who to ask for onsite, the client's PO.
2. Run `node scripts/agency.mjs book "CLIENT" --lang=vi --mode=onsite --setting=medical --at="YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM" --minutes=90 --address="..." [--contact= --for= --po=] --json`.
3. Then straight to `/find` for the interpreter. Court and legal bookings need a Certified Interpreter or above: check before promising.
4. Refused for credit hold: say why. Do not book around it.
