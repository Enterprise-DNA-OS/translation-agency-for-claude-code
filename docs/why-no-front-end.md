# Why there is no front end

XTRF is a database with a subscription. The tables underneath it are ordinary: clients, vendors, projects, jobs, quotes, invoices, a handful of workflows you repeat every week. What you pay for is the layer on top that lets people who do not write SQL get at those tables. Screens, filters, dashboards, forms.

That layer used to be the whole product, because talking to a database was hard. It is not hard any more. Open this folder in Claude Code, describe what you want, and it writes the query, runs it, and explains the answer. Ask a question the dashboard never had a chart for and you still get an answer.

## What you gain

- **Better answers.** A dashboard shows what the vendor decided to chart. Here you ask your own question, in your own words, and get it answered against your own data.
- **No seats.** Every project manager who needs to look can look. The bill does not grow with headcount.
- **Your data in your Postgres.** Plain tables. Back them up, query them from anything, leave any time. There is no export step because there is nothing to leave.
- **A process that matches you.** When your way of working changes, you add a command. You do not wait for a feature request to clear.

## What you give up

- **Client and vendor portals.** Clients email their files and linguists reply to job offers by email. Enterprise DNA builds a portal into your version if you need one.
- **A CAT tool connection.** Word counts are typed in from your CAT tool's analysis, not pulled across. Files live where they live now.
- **An interpreter app.** Timesheets come back on paper or by email and the office enters the minutes.
- **A vendor help desk.** This is open source. Enterprise DNA supports the installed version for agencies that want someone to call.

## Who this fits

Agencies of five to fifty people whose project managers already run the day from email and a spreadsheet beside XTRF. If your clients upload hundreds of files a week through a portal, keep XTRF or have the portal built. If you need the answers more than the screens, this is cheaper, faster and yours.
